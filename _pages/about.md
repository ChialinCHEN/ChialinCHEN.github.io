## Research Experience

### RAG-Based Pediatric Case Analysis

August 2026–present.

Built a hybrid retrieval pipeline using BM25, BGE-M3, and reciprocal rank fusion for pediatric medical literature retrieval. Developed query optimization for medical term mapping, negation preservation, and multi-query reformulation.

## Selected Projects

### Emotion-Aware Music Reasoning

Group course project, summer 2026. [Repository](https://github.com/ZuriZHAO/AIAA3800_Proj).

I implemented the reasoning and music-generation modules. The module turns a fused emotion and fatigue state into a music prompt, comparing a direct emotion map with a theory-of-mind and two-stage chain-of-thought procedure, and synthesizes audio with MusicGen.

### Video Object Removal and Inpainting

Group course project with Qiuyue Liu, spring 2026. [Repository](https://github.com/LIU-Qiuyue/ICVFinalProject).

The pipeline removes dynamic objects from a video and fills the missing regions. The classical baseline uses YOLOv8-Seg, Lucas–Kanade optical flow, and OpenCV inpainting. A second stage uses promptable video segmentation and ProPainter.

### Character Voice Synthesis

Spring 2025.

Built a speech preprocessing pipeline with WebRTC VAD and Whisper, aligned text and audio manually, and fine-tuned F5-TTS for character-specific speech.

### Program Recommendation Agent

Course project. [Repository](https://github.com/taowenxiang/ProgRec).

The system profiles a student and ranks mentors from an academic graph. I integrated the graph-mode agent that runs mentor discovery, teammate discovery, and joint ranking.
